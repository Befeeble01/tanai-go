# TanaiGO

TanaiGO is a Local-First Legal Workspace designed for solo lawyers and small legal teams.

The application helps legal professionals manage clients, cases, documents, tasks, and schedules while keeping data under their control.

Unlike cloud-first solutions, TanaiGO stores data locally and remains fully functional without an internet connection.

## Key Features

* Client Management
* Case Management
* Document Management
* Notes
* Calendar
* Task Management
* Full Text Search
* Local Backup and Restore
* Local-First Architecture

## Product Principles

### Local First

All core functionality works offline.

Your data remains on your device.

### Solo Lawyer First

TanaiGO is designed primarily for independent lawyers and small practices.

### Cloud Optional

Cloud services are optional add-ons and are not required for normal operation.
